Here are the **first questions from each topic**, kept short and interview-friendly:

### 1. Cluster Architecture

**Control plane vs worker nodes:**

* **Control plane:** Manages the cluster, scheduling, API, and desired state.
* **Worker nodes:** Run the actual application workloads (Pods).

### 2. Pods & Containers

**Role of Pods:**

* A **Pod is Kubernetes' smallest deployable unit** and wraps one or more containers that share networking and storage.
* Containers run **inside Pods**.

### 3. Networking & Services

**How does a Service enable communication?**

* A **Service provides a stable IP/DNS name** for a group of Pods and routes traffic to them using label selectors.

### 4. Deployments & Scaling

**What problem does a Deployment solve?**

* A **Deployment manages Pods/ReplicaSets**, providing rolling updates, rollbacks, and maintaining the desired number of replicas.

### 5. Storage & Persistence

**PV + PVC:**

* **PV:** Represents actual persistent storage in the cluster.
* **PVC:** A request for storage that binds to a suitable PV.
* This separates **storage provisioning from application configuration**.

### 6. Configuration Management

**ConfigMaps vs Secrets:**

* **ConfigMap:** Stores non-sensitive configuration.
* **Secret:** Stores sensitive values such as passwords, tokens, and keys.

### 7. Scheduling & Resource Management

**How does the scheduler choose a node?**

* It evaluates eligible nodes based on **resource availability, constraints, affinity/anti-affinity, taints/tolerations, and other scheduling rules**, then selects the best fit.

### 8. Self-Healing & Automation

**How does Kubernetes handle failures?**

* Controllers continuously compare **actual state vs desired state** and recreate failed Pods, reschedule workloads, and maintain the desired replica count.
